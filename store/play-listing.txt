FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
Built for NDT technicians chasing certifications — and built to study the way techs actually live: on shift, on rotation, in places with no signal.

FIVE CERTIFICATION SCHEMES
ASNT (SNT-TC-1A), API (1104/ASME), PCN (BINDT, ISO 9712), CSWIP (TWI), and CGSB — ten modules each, spanning MT, PT, RT, UT and PAUT at Levels I and II. Fundamentals are shared across schemes; codes, acceptance criteria, and certification rules stay scheme-specific, exactly as they should.

REAL EXAM-STYLE QUESTIONS
Hundreds of questions per method — physics, equipment, calibration, codes, and calculations — with rendered diagrams where the exam would have one (V-paths, skip distances, probe geometry) and an Imperial/Metric toggle on calculation questions. Practice sessions assemble fresh forms so you're tested on knowledge, not question memory.

STUDY TOOLS THAT PULL THEIR WEIGHT
• Timed exam-style sessions and casual practice
• Explanations on every question — learn from the miss immediately
• Bookmarks for the ones that keep biting
• History and progress tracking, per question, honestly deduped

FULLY OFFLINE
The whole bank lives on your phone. Fab shops, pipelines, platforms, flights between hitches — no connection needed, ever.

PRIVATE
No account, no cloud, no analytics. Your progress is on your device and nowhere else.

Free to start; one unlock opens the full bank. More certification modules (PAUT, CSWIP, API) are planned as banks are authored.

Study between shifts. Pass the exam. Get the stamp.
